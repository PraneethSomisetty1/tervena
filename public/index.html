Will Tervena diagnose or prescribe?+

Tervena is being built to explain health information and bring relevant changes into the clinical conversation. Our focus is evidence-backed insights that support assessment and follow-up. Diagnosis and treatment decisions remain with the treating professional.

Can a family member participate?+

Patient-controlled family access is central to our design. We’re building for family members who help organize care, understand findings, and prepare questions, whether nearby or in another U.S. city.

How are you approaching doctor and device connections?+

Our integration roadmap starts with one clinic partnership and the records and compatible devices relevant to that care workflow. We’ll expand connections around the needs of patients and their care teams.
