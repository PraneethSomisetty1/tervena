CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Built on questions.
Not overclaims.

What can I try today?+

The interactive demo shows patient connections, a clinician review workspace, and evidence behind illustrative findings. It uses synthetic data and simulated connections. It does not run a live AI model.

Open the demo ↗
Will Tervena diagnose or prescribe?+

Our intended role is to explain information and support clinical review. The proposed analysis would surface changes and possible questions with supporting evidence. Diagnostic or treatment decisions remain with the treating professional. Clinical usefulness has not yet been validated.

Can a family member participate?+

Patient-controlled family access is part of the product vision, including a family member helping from another U.S. city. The patient would choose who can access their information. Family accounts and real sharing are not implemented in the current demo.

Does this work with my doctor or device?+

Real integrations are not available yet. We plan to begin with a clinic partner and the data sources required for one use case. We are not affiliated with Epic or any device manufacturer, and do not claim compatibility with every system.
